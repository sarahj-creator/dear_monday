The week, one day at a time.

The week doesn't have to be something to survive.

Dear Monday explores the small rituals, pauses, and practices that make an ordinary week feel more manageable — and more human. Not a routine to master. Just a gentler way to move through seven ordinary days.

The seven days

A journey through the week

Every day holds something different. Select a day to read what it's for.

Reset Sunday

Preparing for the week without dreading it. Sleep, structure, and a gentler kind of ready — the evening that sets the tone for everything after it.

Dear Monday

The core of it all. Naming the dread, understanding it, and getting through the hardest day honestly — because the research shows it really is harder on your body, not just your mood.

Kind Tuesday

A gentler way to meet Tuesday. Small kindnesses toward a body and mind still recovering from Monday — nothing to fix, just something to tend to.

Check-in Wednesday

A midweek pause. How the week is actually going, and the small adjustments worth making before it's too late to make them.

Hold Thursday

Steadying through the last stretch. Energy, focus, and not burning out before the week is done asking things of you.

Wind-down Friday

Closing the week deliberately. A short ritual for letting go of what the week held, so the weekend can actually start.

Soften Saturday

No agenda. Full rest, a little indulgence, and the one day of the week that asks absolutely nothing of you.
