Meet the week

A 2023 study of over 10,500 heart attack patients across Ireland found the risk of a serious heart attack was 13% higher on a Monday than expected — researchers point to stress hormones and disrupted circadian rhythm tied to the return to work.

British Cardiovascular Society, 2023 — this is why Dear Monday exists.
